# Parchments: The Master User Guide

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for **Parchments**—your intelligent, offline-first companion for Bible study and sermon preparation. This guide is structured according to the six phases of our development journey, providing you with real-world scenarios and step-by-step instructions to master every feature.

---

## 🛠 Phase 1: Foundation & The Core Experience

### User Scenario: The First Session
*Pastor Sarah sits down on a Monday morning to start a new sermon series on the Book of Romans. She needs a clean, fast environment to capture her initial thoughts and organize them into folders.*

### Instructions
1.  **Account Creation:** Launch the app and sign up with your name and email. Since Parchments is offline-first, your credentials are secured locally.
2.  **Organizing your Workspace:** Use the **Left Sidebar** to create your first folder (e.g., "Romans Series").
3.  **Creating your First Note:** Click the **"New Note"** button inside your folder. Parchments will automatically name it based on your first line of text.
4.  **Mastering the Editor:**
    *   Use `Ctrl + B` for Bold and `Ctrl + I` for Italic.
    *   Create a structure using **Headings** (H1 for Titles, H2 for main points).
    *   Parchments auto-saves every 2 seconds, so don't worry about a "Save" button!

---

## 📖 Phase 2: Bible Intelligence

### User Scenario: Deep Exegesis
*Theologian David is studying the Greek nuances of John 1:1. He needs to compare versions and look up original language definitions without switching between books or tabs.*

### Instructions
1.  **Opening the Bible:** Click the **Bible Icon** in the Top Bar to open the Bible columns.
2.  **Parallel Study:** Click the **"+" button** in the Bible header to add up to 4 parallel columns. Use the dropdown to select different versions (e.g., KJV, ESV).
3.  **Interlinear View:** Toggle the **"Interlinear" switch** in any Bible header. You will now see the Greek/Hebrew lemmas directly above the English text.
4.  **Lexicon Lookup:** Click on any Interlinear word to open the **Strong's Lexicon sidebar**. You'll see the definition, part of speech, and pronunciation.
5.  **Cross-References:** Open the **Refs sidebar** to see Treasury of Scripture Knowledge (TSK) links for your current verse.

## 🏗 Phase 3: Study & Composition

### User Scenario: Structuring the Sermon
*Student Marcus is writing a research paper with hundreds of verses. He needs to move sections around and keep his "favorite" verses close at hand.*

### Instructions
1.  **Document Outline:** Open the **Outline sidebar** on the right. Every Heading you create in the editor becomes a clickable link here.
2.  **Structural Reordering:** In the Outline sidebar, **drag and drop** headings to move entire sections of your note instantly.
3.  **Research Bench:** Open the **Research sidebar**. As you find verses in the Bible view, click the **Pin icon** to add them to your Bench.
4.  **Bulk Insertion:** Go to your editor, place the cursor, and click **"Insert All Pins"** in the Research sidebar. Parchments will insert the verses with full citations automatically.

## 🧠 Phase 4: AI & Advanced Intelligence

### User Scenario: The Voice of Inspiration
*While walking, Pastor Sarah has a sudden insight. She records a voice note, and when she returns to her desk, the AI has already transcribed it and suggested relevant Bible verses.*

### Instructions
1.  **Voice Recording:** Click the **Microphone icon** in the Sidebar. Speak your thoughts. 
2.  **Offline Transcription:** Use the **Transcription toggle** in the Voice sidebar. Parchments uses on-device AI (Whisper) to transcribe your audio without sending data to the cloud.
3.  **AI Assistant:** Highlight a paragraph and click the **AI Assistant icon**. Ask it to "Summarize this point" or "Suggest a cross-reference."
4.  **Smart Tagging:** Use the AI to generate tags (e.g., #Grace, #Mercy) based on your note's content for easier searching later.

## 🔄 Phase 5: Sync & Collaboration

### User Scenario: The Study Room
*A small group is studying a passage together. David hosts a "Study Room," and Sarah joins from her tablet. They both see each other's cursors moving in real-time as they annotate the text.*

### Instructions
1.  **Enabling Sync:** Go to **Settings > Sync** and set up your **Private Vault**. This generates your unique encryption keys.
2.  **Sharing a Note:** Click the **Share icon** on any note. Copy the **Room Hash**.
3.  **Real-Time Collaboration:** Your partners can paste the Room Hash in their "Join Room" dialog. You will now see **colored cursors** for every participant.
4.  **E2EE Security:** All data synced between devices is End-to-End Encrypted (AES-256). Only those with the Room Hash and Vault Key can see the content.

---

## 🚀 Phase 6: Distribution & Portability

### User Scenario: Everywhere You Go
*David wants his notes on his Windows desktop, his Android phone, and the church's web terminal.*

### Instructions
1.  **Web (PWA):** Access Parchments via your private URL. Use your browser's "Install" feature to save it as a standalone app.
2.  **Desktop (Tauri):** Download the `.msi` or `.exe` for a native Windows experience with global keyboard shortcuts and file system access.
3.  **Mobile (Capacitor):** Install the Android APK or iOS app. Your data stays local but can sync via the encrypted peer-to-peer network we set up in Phase 5.
4.  **Exporting:** Need to turn your sermon into a handout? Use the **Export menu** to generate a high-quality **PDF** or **DOCX** file.
