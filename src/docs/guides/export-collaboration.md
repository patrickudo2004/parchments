# Exporting, Publishing & Team Collaboration

Parchments makes it easy to move your sermon manuscripts, scholarly essays, and devotional journals out into the world.

---

## 📄 Multi-Format Exporting

Export any note with full typography preservation:

### 1. Microsoft Word (.docx)
* Click **Export > Word Document (.docx)**.
* Produces standard Office Open XML files with clean heading hierarchies, formatted scripture blockquotes, footnotes, and custom author metadata.
* Compatible with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Apple Pages, and LibreOffice.

### 2. Print-Ready PDF (.pdf)
* Click **Export > PDF Document (.pdf)**.
* Automatically applies clean page breaks, margins, header pagination, and publication-ready typography tailored for printouts or electronic distribution.

### 3. Open Markdown (.md)
* Click **Export > Markdown (.md)**.
* Pure CommonMark / GitHub Flavored Markdown with preserved YAML frontmatter, headers, bullet lists, and scripture smart tags.

### 4. HTML & Plain Text (.html / .txt)
* **HTML**: Formatted standalone HTML file with inline CSS styling ready to post to your ministry website or church blog.
* **Plain Text**: Unformatted raw text ready for copying into bulletin announcements or emails.

## 👥 Collaborative Study Spaces

Need to co-author a sermon series, prepare a group Bible study curriculum, or lead a pastoral team?

### 1. Hosting a Study Space
* Click the **Collaborate (👥)** icon on any folder or note.
* Parchments generates a unique **Space Hash**.
* Share this connection code or QR code with your fellow study leaders.
* **Host Authority**: As the host, your local storage acts as the primary source of truth. Changes propagate in real-time across all connected peers.

### 2. Joining a Study Space
* Open the left sidebar and select **"Join Study Space"**.
* Enter the Space Hash provided by the host.
* Parchments connects you into the shared document session, allowing simultaneous typing with live multi-colored collaborative user cursors.

## 🎙️ Group Voice Transcription Sync

During in-person sermon brainstorming meetings:
* Any participant can record voice audio.
* Real-time transcripts stream directly to all joined members' screens.
* Allows your teaching team to capture verbal discussions and immediately shape them into written sermon outlines together.
