# Getting Started & Workspace Basics

Welcome to **Parchments**, a high-performance, offline-first study and sermon preparation environment crafted for scholars, pastors, and serious students of Scripture.

---

## 🏛️ Workspace Philosophy

Parchments operates on three foundational principles:
1. **Total Data Sovereignty**: Your research, sermons, and private journals remain on your physical hardware or secure local browser database—never locked into proprietary cloud silos.
2. **Offline-First Resilience**: Full exegetical study, text editing, Strong's concordance lookup, and cross-referencing function seamlessly without an internet connection.
3. **Ergonomic Simplicity**: A clean, distraction-free writing surface surrounded by rapid-access intelligence panels when you need them.

---

## 🗂️ Opening a Studyspace (Local Folder vs. Browser DB)

When starting with Parchments, you can store your notes in two ways:

### 1. Local Folder Mode (Recommended for Desktop & Web)
* Click the **Folder (📁)** icon in the left explorer or tap **"Open Studyspace"**.
* Select any existing folder on your computer (e.g. `Documents/Sermons`).
* **Zero Lock-In**: Every note you create is saved directly as a standard `.md` (Markdown) file on your hard drive. You can open and edit these files with Obsidian, VS Code, or any text editor.
* Subfolders in your file system are automatically mirrored in Parchments' Explorer tree.

### 2. Browser Database Mode (IndexedDB)
* On mobile browsers or restricted sandboxes where File System Access API is unavailable, notes are automatically persisted in encrypted IndexedDB storage.
* Your work is preserved across browser sessions and cached for instant loading.

## 🖥️ The Tri-Pane Interface

The interface is structured into three distinct ergonomic zones:

### 1. Left Sidebar: Explorer, Outline & Voice
* **Files & Explorer**: Hierarchical folder navigation, note creation, file renaming, and dragging.
* **Outline**: Dynamic table of contents for the active note. Click to jump to any section; drag headings to reshuffle document paragraphs atomically.
* **Voice**: Built-in voice dictation and audio recording shelf.

### 2. Center Writing Surface: Rich Text Editor
* **Rich Text with Markdown Shorthand**: Use standard toolbar controls or quick Markdown tokens (`#`, `##`, `*`, `**`, `>`).
* **Auto-Save Protection**: Real-time background saving ensures no sentence is ever lost.
* **Focus Mode (Zen Writing)**: Isolate a single thought by clicking the Target icon in the Outline to dim all surrounding paragraphs.

### 3. Right Sidebar: Modular Exegetical Bench
* **Bible Reader**: Complete scripture reading pane with parallel translation comparison.
* **Lexicon & Strong's**: Greek and Hebrew dictionary lookups.
* **TSK Cross-References**: 31,102 cross-reference connections from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge.
* **Commentaries & Dictionary**: Historical verse-by-verse expositions and theological definitions.
* **Research Bench (Pins)**: A collection board for pinned verses ready for 1-click insertion into notes.

> [!TIP]
> Both sidebars can be resized by dragging their borders. On mobile and tablet devices, sidebars slide in smoothly and auto-dismiss upon note selection to maximize writing space.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Dictation & Audio Notes

Capture homiletical insights on the go:
* Click the **Voice (🎙️)** button in the Top Bar or Left Sidebar.
* Record audio notes on mobile or desktop; real-time transcription converts spoken words directly into text.
* Review audio with integrated playback controls while refining your sermon notes.
