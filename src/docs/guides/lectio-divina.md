# Lectio Divina & Guided Study Plans

**Lectio Mode** is a dedicated, sacred study environment combining centuries-old Christian contemplative disciplines with modern computational study tools.

---

## 🕊️ What is Lectio Divina?

*Lectio Divina* (Latin for "Divine Reading") is a practice of scriptural reading, meditation, and prayer established in the 6th century by St. Benedict and later formalized by the Carthusian monk Guigo II. 

Unlike analytical exegetical study—which dissects the text—Lectio Divina approaches Scripture sacramentally, treating the Bible as the living voice of God.

## 🪜 The 5-Stage Guided Spiritual Workflow

Parchments enriches the classical four rungs with a fifth incarnational stage:

```
[1. Lectio] ──> [2. Meditatio] ──> [3. Oratio] ──> [4. Contemplatio] ──> [5. Actio]
  (Reading)       (Meditation)       (Prayer)       (Contemplation)      (Action)
```

1. **Lectio (Reading)**: 
   - Attentive, gentle reading of the designated passage.
   - Listening with the "ear of the heart" for a particular word, phrase, or sentence that lights up.
2. **Meditatio (Meditation)**:
   - Ruminating on the chosen word (*ruminatio*—chewing the cud of the word).
   - What does this truth mean in God's redemptive story? Why does it strike your heart today?
3. **Oratio (Prayer)**:
   - Responding to God in honest dialogue based on what was revealed in meditation.
   - Expressing praise, confession, petition, or consecration.
4. **Contemplatio (Contemplation)**:
   - Resting peacefully in the silent presence of God beyond words and intellectual concepts.
   - Receiving God's peace and allowing the truth to permeate the subconscious.
5. **Actio (Action & Incarnation)**:
   - Bridging spiritual contemplation with concrete obedience in daily life.
   - Formulating an intentional commitment: *"How will this Word govern my speech, relationships, and actions today?"*

When you start a daily Lectio session, Parchments automatically generates a structured journal note with these 5 reflective sections ready for your personal entries.

## 📚 6 Supported Study Plan Paradigms

Parchments supports a variety of reading and study disciplines:

1. **The Classical Canonical (One Year)**:
   - Read from Genesis to Revelation chronologically or canonically over 365 days.
2. **The Lectio 24-Chapter Devotional**:
   - Deep immersion across the whole Bible: 10 OT chapters, 10 NT chapters, 2 Psalms, and 2 Proverbs every day.
3. **Single-Book Immersion Plans**:
   - Focus on an individual epistle or narrative with customized endpoints (e.g., *Romans in 16 Days*, *Hebrews in 14 Days*, *Ephesians in 6 Days*).
4. **Curated Topical Journeys**:
   - Multi-day thematic sequences on specialized subjects (e.g., *30 Days on Faith & Trust*, *The Names of God*, *Grief & Hope*).
5. **Word & Theological Concept Studies**:
   - Concentrated plans exploring key biblical terms across testament boundaries (e.g., *Hesed / Lovingkindness*, *Covenant*, *Grace*).
6. **Chronological Historical Sequence**:
   - Read biblical books and pericopes in the historical order in which events occurred (e.g. reading Job during the Patriarchs in Genesis, or Prophets alongside the Books of Kings).

## 🧘 Reading Layouts: Zen Scroll vs. Page-by-Page

Click the **Settings Gear (⚙️)** in the Lectio session header to toggle between:
* **📜 Zen Scroll**: Stacks chapters vertically in a continuous, infinite-scroll view with scripture anchors.
* **📖 Page-by-Page**: Isolates exactly one chapter at a time with a tactile pagination bar (`[Previous Chapter]` / `[Next Chapter]`) to minimize cognitive overload.

## 🔄 The "Grace" Catch-Up Engine

Life happens. If you fall behind on a multi-week or year-long study plan:
* Tap the **"Catch Up"** button on your plan overview.
* The algorithm recalculates your remaining passages and evenly redistributes them over your remaining schedule, keeping your study sustainable and shame-free.

## 📅 Universal Calendar Export (`.ics`)

Export your personalized reading track to any major digital calendar:
* Click **"Export to Calendar (.ics)"**.
* Generates an RFC 5545 standard `.ics` file.
* Import directly into **Google Calendar**, **Apple Calendar**, **Microsoft Outlook**, or **Samsung Calendar** to receive scheduled daily reading reminders with exact chapter assignments.
