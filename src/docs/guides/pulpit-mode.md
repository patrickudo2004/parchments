# Pulpit Mode & Sermon Delivery

**Pulpit Mode** transforms Parchments into an advanced, distraction-free stage prompter and preaching lectern. Built specifically for public delivery, it eliminates window clutter, silences notifications, and provides high-visibility controls optimized for stage lighting and mobile podiums.

---

## 🎙️ Entering & Exiting Pulpit Mode

* **Launch**: Click the **Pulpit Mode** icon in the Top Bar, select **View > Pulpit Presentation Mode**, or tap the Pulpit icon in the mobile actions sheet.
* **Exit**: Click the **Exit (X)** button in the upper-right corner or press `Esc`.

---

## ⚡ Key Stage Capabilities

### 1. In-Pulpit Scripture Quick-Sheet
* **Zero Disruption**: While speaking, you never need to leave your sermon manuscript to look up a verse.
* Tap or click any scripture tag (e.g. `Philippians 4:6-7`) in your preaching notes.
* A high-contrast, non-obtrusive **Scripture Modal / Bottom Sheet** slides up immediately, displaying the full decrypted passage text.
* Includes a 1-tap **Copy** button and a translation switcher (with automatic fallback to KJV if unavailable in the selected version).
* Tap outside or hit `Esc` to instantly return to your exact place in the sermon.

### 2. Lectern Note Switcher
Preaching a service with multiple segments (e.g. *Announcements*, *Main Homily*, *Communion Liturgy*)?
* Tap the sermon title badge in the header: `[📖 Sermon Title ▾]`.
* An instant selector lists all sermon notes in your current folder.
* Switch notes with a single tap without ever exiting presenter mode.

### 3. Pacing & Delivery Modes
Choose between two distinct presentation styles using the mode toggle:
* **📜 Continuous Teleprompter (Scroll)**:
  - Text scrolls automatically at a smooth, customizable velocity.
  - Adjust speed in real-time using the `-` and `+` controls (ranges from 40 to 300 words per minute).
  - Tap anywhere on the stage surface or press `Spacebar` to instantly pause and resume scrolling.
* **📄 Paginated Cards (Paginate)**:
  - Divides your sermon into clean, bite-sized slides or sections based on your headings.
  - Advance or go back with clear, touch-friendly next/previous controls or keyboard arrow keys.

## 🎨 Stage Lighting & Contrast Themes

Pulpit Mode includes three purpose-built color themes to ensure legibility under harsh church spotlights or in dimmed sanctuary auditoriums:

1. **Standard Dark**: Deep charcoal background with crisp off-white typography; ideal for standard tablet and laptop lecterns.
2. **High-Contrast Amber**: Pure OLED pitch-black with radiant warm amber typography; prevents pupil strain and eliminates backlight wash on your face in dark auditoriums.
3. **Clean Light**: High-contrast black text on pure white paper texture; suited for bright outdoor services.

---

## ⏱️ Preacher's Clock & Countdown Timer

* **Elapsed Time**: Keep track of total elapsed preaching time directly from the header.
* **Countdown Mode**: Set target time limits (e.g. 25 minutes, 35 minutes). As time winds down, visual cues gently alert you without flashing intrusive alarms.
* **Reset & Pause**: Tap the timer pill to pause or reset the timer.

---

## 🎮 Hardware Remotes & Stage Hotkeys

Pulpit Mode responds natively to standard wireless presenter remotes and foot pedals:

| Key / Hardware Action | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Spacebar` | Pause / Resume Teleprompter Auto-Scroll |
| `Arrow Right` / `Page Down` | Next Page / Increase Teleprompter Speed |
| `Arrow Left` / `Page Up` | Previous Page / Decrease Teleprompter Speed |
| `Esc` | Safely Exit Pulpit Mode |
| `+` / `-` | Enlarge / Reduce Presentation Font Size |
