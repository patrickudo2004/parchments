# Scripture Intelligence & Exegetical Research

Parchments bridges the gap between devotional reflection and deep academic exegesis by turning every scripture reference into an interactive research portal.

---

## ⚡ Smart Scripture Recognition

While drafting sermon manuscripts or study notes, you do not need to pause your writing flow to copy and paste Bible verses.

### In-Text Citation Detection
* Simply type any standard biblical reference:
  - `John 3:16`
  - `Romans 8:28-30`
  - `Psalm 23:1, 4-6`
  - `1 Cor 13:4-8`
* Parchments detects the citation dynamically and wraps it in a **Smart Scripture Tag**.
* **Hover Preview**: Moving your cursor over the citation on desktop displays a tooltip with the decrypted passage text.
* **Click to Inspect**: Clicking or tapping the tag opens the passage directly in the Bible Study Panel or in-context sheet without losing your cursor placement.

---

## 🏛️ Original Language Lexicon & Strong's Concordance

Uncover original Hebrew (Old Testament) and Greek (New Testament) root meanings without flipping through voluminous printed lexicons:

1. **Strong's Tagging**: In the Bible Reader, enable Interlinear or Strong's view to see Strong's numbers (e.g. `H430` for *Elohim*, `G26` for *Agape*) aligned with each word.
2. **Lemma & Transliteration**: Click any Strong's number to view:
   - Root lemma in original script (Biblical Hebrew / Koine Greek).
   - Accurate transliteration and pronunciation guide.
   - Derivation and etymological roots.
   - Exhaustive definitions, translation counts, and occurrences across the biblical canon.
3. **Quick Lookup**: Press `Ctrl + K` and type `strongs:G26` or open **Tools > Strong's Lookup** at any time.

## 🔗 Treasury of Scripture Knowledge (TSK) Cross-References

With over **31,102 verses indexed**, the built-in TSK cross-reference engine helps scripture interpret scripture:

* Open the **TSK References** sidebar tab (Cross icon ✝️).
* The panel automatically aligns with the active verse in your reader or note cursor.
* Browse thematic cross-references categorized by keyword or doctrinal concept.
* Click any connected reference to instantly inspect the parallel passage.

> [!NOTE]
> All reference datasets are indexed and ingested into IndexedDB in non-blocking batches. Your UI remains buttery-smooth even while querying tens of thousands of biblical connections.

## 📖 Commentaries & Historical Dictionaries

1. **Verse Commentaries**: Access classic expositions (e.g., Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown) synchronized with your current chapter.
2. **Theological Dictionaries**: Look up cultural concepts, geographic places, weights and measures, and historical context via Easton's Bible Dictionary.
3. **Direct Search**: Search for terms directly within the Dictionary panel to study concepts independently of reader navigation.

---

## 📌 The Research Bench (Persistent Pins)

When synthesizing a multi-passage sermon or research paper:
1. Tap the **Pin (📌)** icon beside any verse in the Bible Reader.
2. The verse is collected into your **Research Bench** (accessible in the right sidebar).
3. Pins persist as you navigate across Genesis, Psalms, or the Epistles.
4. When ready to write, click **"Insert All"** or drag individual verses directly into your sermon manuscript as beautifully styled blockquotes.

---

## 📥 Managing Bible Translations & Offline Catalog

* Navigate to **Settings > Translations**.
* Download official public-domain and open-license translations directly for offline storage (KJV, ASV, BBE, WEB, YLT, and original language texts).
* **Graceful Translation Fallbacks**: If an active translation lacks a particular testament (such as Greek TR for Old Testament passages), Parchments gracefully displays the verified KJV text with a distinct version indicator badge to prevent broken previews.
